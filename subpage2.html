Folklore and Traditions

Halloween has its roots in the ancient Celtic festival of Samhain, which marked the end of the harvest season and the beginning of winter.

Trick-or-Treating

The tradition of trick-or-treating dates back to the early 20th century in the United States, where children would go door-to-door asking for treats.

Costumes

Wearing costumes on Halloween is a tradition that has evolved over time, with people dressing up as various characters, from ghosts and witches to superheroes and pop culture icons.
